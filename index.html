Why we work this way

RTAid Foundation was founded to close the gap between emergency relief and long-term recovery — the period where the crisis has faded from headlines but a community is still rebuilding.

We don't run one program in isolation. A farming family recovering from displacement needs seed and tools, but also a safe environment for their children and a school that's still open.

That's why our four programmes — Agricultural Recovery, Education, Protection, and Food Security & Livelihoods — are designed and delivered together, in the same communities, by the same field teams.

Our programmes

Four connected areas of work, run together in every community we serve.

Explore 4 connected programmes
Agricultural Recovery & Development

Getting farms producing again

We provide seed, tools, and training to farming households who've lost a season or more to displacement or disaster, paired with support to restore degraded land and diversify what's grown.

Education

Keeping children learning through disruption

We rebuild and equip classrooms, train teachers working in displacement-affected areas, and run catch-up learning for children who've missed months or years of school.

Protection

Keeping the most vulnerable safe

Our caseworkers identify and support survivors of violence and exploitation, reunite separated children with their families, and run community awareness on safe reporting channels.

Food Security & Livelihoods (FSL)

Bridging the gap until harvest

Cash transfers, food assistance, and small-business grants for households with no current income — sized and timed to cover the gap until a farm or trade is producing again.
